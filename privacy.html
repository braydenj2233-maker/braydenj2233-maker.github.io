Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

This policy explains what information the Knockout IQ game ("the game", "we", "us") handles on iPhone and Android, why, and the choices you have. In short: your game progress stays on your device (with a backup in your own iCloud on iPhone), we don't run accounts or servers, the ads in the game come from Google AdMob, which collects some information to show and measure ads, and anonymous play stats go to Unity Analytics (you can turn those off).

1. Information kept on your device and in your iCloud

The game saves your progress in a file on your device. It includes:

  • the boxer name you type (we suggest not using your real name) and your boxer's color;
  • coins, JK vouchers, wins and losses, career stars and belts, nicknames;
  • the cosmetics, stages and word packs you own and wear;
  • Study mode progress, including questions you missed and any custom word packs you make;
  • settings like sound and music, and whether you bought Remove Ads or the Gym Membership.

On iPhone, a copy of this file is also kept in your own iCloud account (Apple's iCloud key-value storage), so your progress comes back if you reinstall the game or move to a new iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account. That copy is stored by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we can't see it. If iCloud is turned off for the game in your iPhone's Settings, the save stays on your device only.

We do not receive this file. It is not sent to us or shared with anyone.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on some screens, an occasional full-screen ad between matches, and videos you can choose to watch for coins. To show, limit and measure ads, Google may collect information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (the IDFA on iPhone, only if you allow tracking; the Android advertising ID on Android);
  • your IP address and the general location it suggests;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • how you interact with ads (views and taps) and crash or performance information about the ads.

Ads in the game are limited to a general-audience ("G") content rating.

Your choices:

  • iPhone: the game asks once whether it may track you for ads. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not personalized using your IDFA. You can change this any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings (usually Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Google > Ads).
  • Europe, the UK and Switzerland: the game shows Google's consent message before ads. You can change your answers any time from the game's Settings > Privacy Choices.
  • Remove Ads: buying Remove Ads turns off banners and full-screen ads between matches. Videos you choose to watch for coins stay available.

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Purchases

Purchases (the Gym Membership, JK voucher packs and Remove Ads) are handled by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see your name, card or payment details. The store tells the game which items you own so it can unlock them, and so Restore Purchases works.

4. Game stats (Unity Analytics)

The game is made with Unity, and purchases use Unity's in-app purchasing library. To learn which parts of the game players enjoy and where they get stuck, the game sends anonymous play stats to Unity Analytics:

  • which screens you open, and which fights you start, finish, win, lose or leave, with how much health was left and whether you used a revive;
  • Unity's own session information, such as how long and how often you play, your device model, operating system and language, a random ID Unity makes for this install, the general location suggested by your IP address, and in-app purchases you make.

We don't send your boxer name, your answers or anything that says who you are, and we don't use these stats for ads. You can turn them off any time in the game's Settings > Share Game Stats. See Unity's privacy policy for how Unity handles this information.

5. Children

Knockout IQ is meant for players 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we will help remove it.

6. Keeping and deleting information

Your progress lives on your device until you delete the game, and on iPhone in your iCloud until you remove it (iPhone Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage Storage, or turn iCloud off for the game). Information collected by Google for ads is handled under Google's policies; you can limit it with the choices in section 2. Because we don't hold your game data, we can't restore lost progress for you, but purchases come back with Restore Purchases.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to opt out of its use for personalized ads. Since we don't store your information, most requests are handled by deleting the game and using the ad choices above. You can also email us and we'll help.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.

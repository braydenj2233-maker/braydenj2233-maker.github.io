Support

Stuck, found a wrong answer, or something isn't working? Email knockoutiq.support@gmail.com. Tell us your phone model and what happened, and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

I bought something and it's missing.

Open the game's Settings (the gear on the main screen) and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with. Coins and JK vouchers you already received can't be restored, but the Gym Membership and Remove Ads can.

I got a new phone or reinstalled. Where's my progress?

Your progress is saved on your phone only, so deleting the game or changing phones starts a new career. Purchases you own for good come back with Restore Purchases.

How do I turn off ads?

Buy Remove Ads on the Shop's JK Vouchers shelf. It turns off the banner and the ads between matches. Videos you choose to watch for coins stay available.

A "watch an ad" button didn't give me coins.

Coins come after the whole video plays. If no ad is ready, wait a moment and try again; a weak internet connection can stop ads from loading.
